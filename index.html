Our heritage

A legacy woven
in Indian tradition

Founded in 1980 in the heart of Aligarh, Hers Cottons has been a destination for women who care about the art of Indian ethnic dressing. For over four decades we have kept the same brief: honour the craft, and let the cloth speak.

Every thread carries a story — of master kaarigars, of silhouettes that have outlasted every trend, and of the quiet confidence that comes from wearing something rooted in your own culture.

The beginning

Born from Aligarh's
cultural soul

Aligarh is no ordinary city. Home to Aligarh Muslim University, one of India's most storied centres of learning, it has long been a place of culture, poetry and refinement — where women have walked the corridors of academia dressed in silhouettes that state identity as clearly as intellect.

Hers Cottons was founded in that spirit in 1980 by Munir Iqbal Siddiqui, who understood that what a woman wears carries more than fabric. It carries her roots, her bearing, and her story. He set out to build a place where the women of Aligarh could find ethnic wear that honoured tradition while meeting the demands of an educated, confident, modern life.

From fine cotton suit pieces to ajrakh and kalamkari that carry the mark of the hand that made them, every piece is chosen with the same intent: to celebrate who we are and where we come from.
