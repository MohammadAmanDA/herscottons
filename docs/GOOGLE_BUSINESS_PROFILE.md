# Google Business Profile — what to put in it

The listing is claimed and verified. This is the copy to paste in, and the
order to do it in.

Everything here uses only facts the owner has confirmed. Nothing about
tailoring, delivery, pricing, stock counts or years of experience appears
unless it was stated. If a field wants something not listed here, ask rather
than fill it plausibly — a wrong detail on Google is worse than a blank one,
because customers act on it.

---

## 1. Check what Google already has

The listing existed before it was claimed, so some fields were filled by Google
from public data and may be wrong. Verify each before adding anything:

| Field | Correct value |

| Phone | +91 92670 12751 |
| Hours | Sat–Thu 11:00–21:00, **Friday closed** |

costs you a wasted customer journey.

## 2. Website field

Paste exactly this, including everything after the question mark:

```
https://herscottons.pages.dev/?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp
```

The tags are invisible to customers and change nothing about the page. They are
what later lets you separate "found us on Google Maps" from "came from
Instagram" instead of guessing.

---

## 3. Categories

Primary category decides which searches you appear in, so it matters more than
any other single field.

- **Primary:** `Fabric store` — matches unstitched suit pieces, which is what
  the shop actually sells. `Clothing store` is broader but pulls in
  ready-to-wear searches the shop cannot serve.
- **Secondary:** `Women's clothing store`, `Boutique`

Google only offers categories from its own fixed list. If `Fabric store` is not
offered, the nearest match is what to take.

## 4. Description (max 750 characters)

```
Hers Cottons has dressed the women of Aligarh since 1980.

We stock unstitched suit pieces across a wide range of Indian ethnic fabric —
ajrakh, kalamkari, bandhani, chikankari, batik, khadi, block print and handloom

Founded in 1980 by Munir Iqbal Siddiqui and run by the same family for over

Open Saturday to Thursday, 11am to 9pm. Closed Fridays.

See what has just come in at instagram.com/herscottons
```

Google shows roughly the first 250 characters before "more", so the year and
what is sold both sit in the visible part deliberately.

## 5. Photos

Upload the whole optimised library from `assets/img/`. Order matters — the
first few are what people see:

1. `storepic` — the shopfront with the sign. Google uses this for the
   "is this the right place?" moment.
2. `owner2` — the founder portrait, under Team
3. The fabric shots, as Products

Then keep adding **1–2 photos a week, permanently**. Recency is weighted
heavily and almost every local shop uploads once and stops. This is the
cheapest ranking advantage available.

## 7. Reviews

The short link is live: **herscottons.pages.dev/review** (or `/r`).

The printable QR is at `assets/print/review-qr.svg`. It encodes our short link,
not Google's URL, so when the profile's official review link is available it is
a one-line change in `_redirects` and nothing already printed is wasted.

**Once you have the official link** from the dashboard ("Ask for reviews", of
the form `https://g.page/r/XXXX/review`), update the `/review` target. That one
opens the star dialog directly instead of the listing.

Rules that matter:

- **Never offer anything in exchange for a review.** Explicit Google policy
  violation. Penalties run from every review being deleted to suspension.
- **Pace it.** 5–15 a day, steadily. A profile going from ~0 to 30 a day looks
  bought, and the spam filter removes them silently — the work is done and the
  reviews vanish.
- Ask at the counter, right after the sale, while she is happy.
- Ask in Hindi or Urdu. Reviews in any language count fully.
- Encourage a photo and a sentence. Star-only reviews carry far less weight.
- **Reply to every review within 24 hours**, including bad ones. Replies are a
  ranking signal and a calm reply to a 3-star reads better to a stranger than
  a wall of identical 5-stars.

## 8. What to watch

In the profile's Performance tab:

- **Searches** — discovery (found by category) vs direct (searched the name).
  Discovery growing means the categories and photos are working.
- **Direction requests** — the closest thing to intent to visit.
- **Calls** and **website clicks**.

On the site side, the `utm_campaign=gbp` tag separates Google traffic from
Instagram once analytics is enabled.

Check monthly, not daily. Local ranking moves slowly and daily numbers are
noise.
